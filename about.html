About Me

Hi, I'm Niniola Fathia.

I'm someone who loves words, stories, and the little things that sometimes become difficult to say out loud.

I created this website as a place to share my first personal story, The Girl I Used to Be.

This story is a small piece of my journey, and writing it gave me a way to put some of the things I felt into words.

This is only the beginning of my writing journey.
